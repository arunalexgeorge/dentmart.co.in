Our Story

Precision Crafted, Patient Centered

Dentmart Dental Lab is founded on the belief that premium dental prosthetics should be accessible to every practice — whether a solo clinic in a small town or a bustling urban dental center.

Based in Koothattukulam, Ernakulam, our team combines traditional craftsmanship with the latest CAD/CAM, 3D printing, and direct metal laser sintering (DMLS) technology. Every restoration is fabricated with meticulous attention to fit, form, and function.

Our Lab & Technology

Precision Equipment, Proven Results

We invest in the latest dental manufacturing technology to ensure every restoration meets the highest standards of fit, function, and esthetics.

5-Axis Milling

XTCERA X-Mill 500 Plus

High-precision 5-axis dental milling machine for zirconia, PMMA, and wax — delivering exceptional accuracy and surface finish.

3D Scanning

SHINING 3D AutoScan DS-EX Pro

Dual 5.0 MP camera lab scanner capturing every detail of dies, models, and articulators with ≤ 8 μm accuracy.

Porcelain Firing

Ivoclar Programat P310

State-of-the-art ceramic furnace with QTK2 muffle technology and color touchscreen for flawless porcelain and glaze firing.

Sintering

ZTCF-30B Zirconia Sintering Furnace

High-temperature sintering oven designed specifically for dental zirconia — ensuring full density and optimal flexural strength.

3D Printing

ELEGOO Saturn 4 12K

Ultra-high-resolution resin 3D printer for dental models, surgical guides, and temporaries with 19×24 micron XY resolution.

Intraoral Scanning

SHINING 3D Aoralscan 3

High-performance intraoral scanner offering exceptional accuracy, speed, and AI-powered scanning for an enhanced digital workflow.
