Authentication Policy

Our Commitment & Coverage

Please read these terms carefully. They outline the scope of our authentication coverage and claim procedures.

The authentication covers only manufacturing defects, subject to evaluation by Dentmart's experts.

Damages caused by accidents, misuse, or negligence are not covered. The authentication allows only for replacement of the defective prosthesis; no compensation is provided for breakage.

Authentication claims must be submitted with a certified doctor's note, the damaged prosthesis, new jaw models, and the original authentication certificate. Incomplete claims may be rejected.

The authentication does not cover damage to surrounding oral structures (such as teeth, gums, or bone).

Claims must be made through the registered clinic. Patients are required to follow post-treatment care instructions and attend regular dental check-ups every six months or as advised.

The authentication excludes consultation fees, X-rays, further treatments, modifications, models, temporary work, transportation, and placement. Changes to the original design are not permitted.

Issues related to extensions, occlusion, and fit are based on the original model. The final prosthesis may not exactly replicate the original.

Dentmart may update authentication terms via its website. All disputes fall under the jurisdiction of the Ernakulam District Court, Kerala.
