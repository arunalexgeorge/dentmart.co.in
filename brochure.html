Authentication Policy — Please read carefully before placing your order

Authentication
Coverage

Authentication covers manufacturing defects only, subject to evaluation by Dentmart's experts.

Damages from accidents, misuse, or negligence are not covered. Only replacement of defective prosthesis — no cash compensation.

Claims must include: doctor's note, damaged prosthesis, new jaw models, and original authentication certificate.

Does not cover damage to surrounding oral structures (teeth, gums, or bone).

Claims must be made through the registered clinic. Patients must follow post-treatment care and attend check-ups every 6 months.

Excludes: consultation fees, X-rays, modifications, transportation, and placement. Original design changes not permitted.

Extension, occlusion, and fit issues are based on original model. Final prosthesis may not exactly replicate the original.

Dentmart may update terms via dentmart.co.in. All disputes under jurisdiction of Ernakulam District Court, Kerala.
