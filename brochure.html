Care Protocol

Patient Co-Responsibility

Important Guidelines
Patient Co-Responsibility & Care Protocol

To ensure the long-term clinical success, structural integrity, and durability of your advanced dental restoration, the following medical guidelines must be strictly followed.

Mandatory Care Guidelines

These guidelines are essential for maintaining your dental restoration

Patient
Responsibility
1. Mandatory 6-Month Check-ups

You must visit your dentist at least twice a year (every 6 months) for a routine clinical evaluation and professional cleaning to monitor the health of the restoration and surrounding teeth.

2. Strict Oral Hygiene Standards

In addition to brushing twice daily, you must consistently use an Interdental Brush, Floss threaders, or a Water Flosser to clean beneath and around the margins of your crowns and bridges. This is absolutely critical to prevent secondary decay at the root and gum disease.

3. Avoid Abnormal Forces

Do not subject your restoration to unnatural loading. Avoid biting nails, tearing open plastic packaging, or chewing on exceptionally hard items like ice cubes, bones, hard candy, or nutshells. Artificial restorations should not be subjected to more stress than natural teeth.

4. Compulsory Nightguard Use

Patients diagnosed with a history of bruxism (teeth grinding) or clenching must wear a professionally fabricated nightguard every night. This protects the delicate ceramic layers from fracturing under heavy, unconscious bite forces.

5. Exclusion of Biological Changes & Trauma

The dental laboratory cannot be held responsible for structural failures caused by underlying biological changes — such as progressive bone loss (periodontitis), recurrent decay in the supporting root, or systemic conditions like uncontrolled diabetes — as well as external accidents or facial trauma.

6. Unauthorized Alterations

Any adjustments, grinding, reshaping, or repairs performed on this prosthesis by another dental clinic or unauthorized practitioner without consulting your treating dentist will immediately void all care and maintenance terms.

By accepting your dental restoration from Dentmart, you acknowledge and agree to follow these care guidelines. Non-compliance may affect the validity of your authentication coverage.
