Authentication Policy

Our Commitment & Coverage

Please read these terms carefully. They outline the scope of our authentication coverage and claim procedures.

The authentication covers only manufacturing defects, subject to evaluation by Dentmart's experts.

Damages caused by accidents, misuse, or negligence are not covered. The authentication allows only for replacement of the defective prosthesis; no compensation is provided for breakage.

Authentication claims must be submitted with a certified doctor's note, the damaged prosthesis, new jaw models, and the original authentication certificate. Incomplete claims may be rejected.

The authentication does not cover damage to surrounding oral structures (such as teeth, gums, or bone).

Claims must be made through the registered clinic. Patients are required to follow post-treatment care instructions and attend regular dental check-ups every six months or as advised.

The authentication excludes consultation fees, X-rays, further treatments, modifications, models, temporary work, transportation, and placement. Changes to the original design are not permitted.

Issues related to extensions, occlusion, and fit are based on the original model. The final prosthesis may not exactly replicate the original.

Dentmart may update authentication terms via its website. All disputes fall under the jurisdiction of the Ernakulam District Court, Kerala.

Care Protocol

Patient Co-Responsibility & Care Protocol

To ensure the long-term clinical success, structural integrity, and durability of your advanced dental restoration, the following medical guidelines must be strictly followed.

Mandatory 6-Month Check-ups: You must visit your dentist at least twice a year (every 6 months) for a routine clinical evaluation and professional cleaning to monitor the health of the restoration and surrounding teeth.

Strict Oral Hygiene Standards: In addition to brushing twice daily, you must consistently use an Interdental Brush, Floss threaders, or a Water Flosser to clean beneath and around the margins of your crowns and bridges. This is absolutely critical to prevent secondary decay at the root and gum disease.

Avoid Abnormal Forces: Do not subject your restoration to unnatural loading. Avoid biting nails, tearing open plastic packaging, or chewing on exceptionally hard items like ice cubes, bones, hard candy, or nutshells. Artificial restorations should not be subjected to more stress than natural teeth.

Compulsory Nightguard Use: Patients diagnosed with a history of bruxism (teeth grinding) or clenching must wear a professionally fabricated nightguard every night. This protects the delicate ceramic layers from fracturing under heavy, unconscious bite forces.

Exclusion of Biological Changes & Trauma: The dental laboratory cannot be held responsible for structural failures caused by underlying biological changes — such as progressive bone loss (periodontitis), recurrent decay in the supporting root, or systemic conditions like uncontrolled diabetes — as well as external accidents or facial trauma.

Unauthorized Alterations: Any adjustments, grinding, reshaping, or repairs performed on this prosthesis by another dental clinic or unauthorized practitioner without consulting your treating dentist will immediately void all care and maintenance terms.
